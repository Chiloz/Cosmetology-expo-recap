Zambia's Premier Beauty & Business Showcase

Cosmetology Expo Zambia 2026 was the country's leading platform for beauty, skincare, hair innovation, and wellness entrepreneurship. It brought together professionals, students, brands, and innovators shaping the future of the beauty industry in Africa.

Attendees experienced live demonstrations, networking opportunities, product launches, masterclasses, and exhibitions from top local and international brands.
